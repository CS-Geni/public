01 / Why we are here

Talent is everywhere.
Clear pathways are not.

We want computer science education to feel less like a gate and more like an invitation.

CS-Geni’s mission is to help learners move from “What does that mean?” to “I can build that”—with plain language, visual practice, useful feedback, and room to grow at their own pace.
